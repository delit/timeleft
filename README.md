# ⏳ Time Left to Live

**A personal life expectancy countdown — because time is the one thing you can't get back.**

---

## What does it do?

*Time Left to Live* shows you how much time you have left based on the average life expectancy for your country and gender.

You enter three things:

1. **Your country** — choose from all countries in the world
2. **Your birth year** — from 1925 up to last year
3. **Your gender** — male or female

Hit **Calculate**, and the app gives you a real-time countdown of your remaining time — ticking down every second.

---

## What you see

- **Live countdown** — days, hours, minutes and seconds remaining
- **Big picture** — total months and weeks left
- **Pie chart** — a visual split between how much of your life you've lived and how much remains
- **Milestones** — how long until your next significant birthdays (e.g. 70, 80, 90)
- **Full moons remaining** — because why not
- **Summers remaining** — a gentle reminder that summers are precious

All data is based on **WHO (World Health Organization) life expectancy figures** and updates automatically in real time.

---

## A note on the numbers

This is a statistical average — not a prediction. Plenty of people live far beyond the average, and plenty don't reach it. Think of it less as a countdown to death, and more as a reminder to make the most of the time you have.

---

## Tech

A single-page web app. No server, no tracking, no account needed. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your settings are saved so the countdown picks up where it left off next time you open the page.
